04 / BEFORE YOUR VISIT

24 hours before.

A little prep for the perfect glam ♡

Gently exfoliate the evening before to remove dry, flaky skin. Avoid harsh scrubs.

Use your usual moisturiser and drink plenty of water.

Avoid new skincare products, peels, retinol or strong acids shortly before your appointment.

If you plan to wax or thread, please do so 24–48 hours beforehand, rather than just before your appointment.

Please arrive without makeup if possible. Your usual skincare is absolutely fine.

Send 1–3 inspiration photos of your preferred makeup look 24 hours beforehand, so I can prepare and tailor your glam to your features.

If possible, wear a top with a similar colour and neckline to your outfit so we can create a cohesive look.

Thank you for helping us make the most of your appointment.
